Privacy Policy

Information we receive

When you contact us, request a consultation or submit a form, we may receive your name, email address, contact details and information you choose to provide about your matter. Our website may also receive technical information such as an IP address and browser information through its hosting and service providers.

How we use and share information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange and provide services, process transactions, maintain the website and meet applicable legal obligations. We may share information with service providers involved in hosting, scheduling, email or payment processing as needed for those purposes, or where required by law.

Payment information

Payments may be handled by a payment service provider or your UPI application. Please review the relevant provider’s privacy terms. Do not send card details, UPI PINs or passwords by email.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information and retain it as needed to provide services, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive case documents until we have discussed an appropriate method.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, email iam@rks.ad. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law and professional obligations.
