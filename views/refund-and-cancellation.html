Refund & Cancellation Policy

Research and preparation

Consultations and related services involve research, preliminary preparation and professional effort. Fees paid for these services are generally non-refundable, including if you cancel or do not attend, subject to applicable law and any rights that cannot be excluded.

One reschedule

One reschedule may be requested for a booking by emailing iam@rks.ad. The request must be made within 24 hours after booking or payment and at least 24 hours before the appointment. Rescheduling is subject to availability and confirmation. Please include your booking details and preferred alternative time.

Payment issues

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, paid twice, or experienced a failed transaction, email us with the transaction reference and relevant details. We will review the issue and respond as appropriate, including where a reversal or refund is required by law or payment-provider rules. Do not email sensitive payment credentials.

How to contact us

Send cancellation, rescheduling or payment enquiries to iam@rks.ad. This policy applies to services and does not describe returns of physical goods.
